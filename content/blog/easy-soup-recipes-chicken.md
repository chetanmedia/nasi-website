The easiest chicken soup starts with three things: a good broth, cooked chicken and a few soft vegetables. Simmer onion, carrot and celery in broth until tender, stir in shredded chicken, add noodles or rice, and season. That base takes about 30 minutes, and once you know it, you can turn it into almost any chicken soup you like.

Below is the base method, then 20 easy variations grouped by how much time you have and what you are in the mood for.

## What do you need for an easy chicken soup?

Most chicken soups share the same short list. Get these right and the rest is flavor.

### The chicken

You have three easy routes:

- **Rotisserie chicken.** The fastest. Shred the meat and add it near the end so it just warms through.
- **Boneless thighs.** Simmer them whole in the broth for about 20 minutes, then shred. They stay tender.
- **Bone-in pieces.** Take longer, around 40 minutes, but give the richest broth.

Whatever you use, raw chicken needs to reach 165°F (74°C) inside. An instant-read thermometer takes the guessing out.

### The broth

Store-bought chicken broth is fine. Choose low-sodium so you control the salt. Plan on about 8 cups for a pot that serves 4 to 6.

### The vegetables and aromatics

Onion, carrot and celery are the classic trio. Garlic, a bay leaf and a sprig of thyme add depth. Soft greens like spinach go in at the very end.

### The filler

Egg noodles, small pasta, rice, potatoes or beans. Each one makes the soup heartier.

## How do you make chicken soup? (the base method)

1. Warm 2 tablespoons of oil or butter in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add 1 diced onion, 2 diced carrots and 2 diced celery stalks with a pinch of salt. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes until soft.
3. Stir in 2 or 3 minced garlic cloves and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Pour in 8 cups of broth, add a bay leaf and some thyme, and bring to a simmer.
5. If using raw thighs, add them now and simmer about 20 minutes until cooked through. Lift them out and shred.
6. Return the chicken to the pot (or add rotisserie chicken now). Add your filler and cook until tender.
7. Taste. Add salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon. Finish with parsley or dill.

That is it. Every idea below is a twist on this pattern.

## What are the best easy chicken soup ideas?

These are described in general terms, so you can build each one from the base above.

### When you have 30 minutes or less

1. **Rotisserie chicken noodle.** The base with egg noodles and lots of fresh dill.
2. **Chicken and rice.** Use leftover cooked rice and stir it in at the end so it stays fluffy.
3. **Chicken tortellini.** Fresh cheese tortellini, a handful of spinach and parmesan on top.
4. **Lemon chicken orzo.** Orzo cooked in the broth, finished with plenty of lemon juice.
5. **Ginger garlic chicken.** A simple brothy soup with sliced ginger, garlic, soy sauce and green onions.
6. **Chicken tortilla.** Broth with salsa, black beans and corn, topped with crushed tortilla chips and avocado.

### When you want something creamy

7. **Creamy chicken and wild rice.** A little flour and milk or cream to thicken, plus wild rice for chew.
8. **Chicken pot pie soup.** Creamy base with peas and carrots, served with biscuits or pastry on the side.
9. **Chicken corn chowder.** Potatoes and corn in a creamy broth, finished with chives.
10. **White chicken chili.** White beans, green chiles and cumin, with a swirl of sour cream.
11. **Creamy Tuscan-style chicken.** Sun-dried tomatoes, spinach and parmesan in a creamy broth.

### When you want to set it and forget it

12. **Slow cooker chicken noodle.** Cook the chicken and vegetables low and slow, add noodles near the end.
13. **Slow cooker chicken taco soup.** Chicken, beans, tomatoes and taco spices, then shred and top.
14. **Pressure cooker chicken and vegetable.** Bone-in pieces give a rich broth in a fraction of the time.
15. **Chicken and dumplings.** Simmer the soup, then drop spoonfuls of simple dumpling dough on top and cover.

### When you are feeling under the weather

16. **Simple chicken and egg drop.** Hot broth with ribbons of beaten egg stirred in, plus shredded chicken.
17. **Chicken and ginger congee-style rice.** Rice cooked long in broth until soft and porridge-like.
18. **Turmeric chicken and vegetable.** A golden broth with ginger, turmeric and carrots.

### When you want bold flavor

19. **Coconut curry chicken.** Curry paste and coconut milk, finished with lime and cilantro.
20. **Chicken and white bean with pesto.** A bright, brothy soup with a spoon of pesto stirred into each bowl.

## How do you store and reheat chicken soup safely?

Soup makes great leftovers, as long as you handle it well.

- **Cool it fast.** Divide a big pot into shallow containers so it cools quickly. Refrigerate within 2 hours.
- **Fridge:** eat within 3 to 4 days.
- **Freezer:** freeze for up to about 3 months. Leave room at the top of the container, since liquid expands.
- **Keep noodles separate.** Pasta and rice soak up broth overnight. Store them apart, or cook a fresh batch when you reheat.
- **Reheat until steaming.** Bring it back to a simmer on the stove, or heat in the microwave until hot all the way through.

Cream-based soups can separate a little after freezing. Stir well as you reheat, or freeze the soup before adding the cream and stir the cream in later.
