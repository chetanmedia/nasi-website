The best yam Thanksgiving recipes are really sweet potato recipes: classic casserole with pecans or marshmallows, stovetop candied yams, savory mash with garlic and herbs, or roasted wedges with a hot honey glaze. Most can be prepped a day or two ahead, and several never need the oven at all. Below are 24 ideas grouped by style, plus how to tell yams from sweet potatoes, how much to buy and how to fit it all around the turkey.

## Are yams and sweet potatoes the same thing?

Not quite, but for Thanksgiving it rarely matters.

### What US stores call "yams"

The orange, moist, sweet tubers labeled "yams" in most American supermarkets are a type of sweet potato. Decades ago, growers started calling the soft, orange-fleshed varieties "yams" to tell them apart from the paler, firmer sweet potatoes. The name stuck. Canned "yams" are almost always sweet potatoes too.

### What a true yam is

True yams are a different plant. They have rough, bark-like skin, white or purple flesh and a starchier, less sweet taste. You will usually find them at African, Caribbean or Asian markets. They are delicious boiled, fried or mashed, but they do not behave like sweet potatoes in a sugary casserole.

### Which should you buy?

For every idea in this post, buy orange sweet potatoes (whatever the sign says). Look for firm ones with smooth skin and no soft spots. Pick similar sizes so they cook evenly.

## What are the classic sweet potato dishes for Thanksgiving?

These are the ones people ask for by name. If you only make one, make one of these.

1. **Sweet potato casserole with marshmallows.** Buttery mashed sweet potatoes under a toasted marshmallow lid. The kid favorite.
2. **Sweet potato casserole with pecan streusel.** Same base, topped with brown sugar, butter and chopped pecans that bake into a crunchy crust.
3. **Half-and-half casserole.** Marshmallows on one side, pecans on the other. It ends the family debate.
4. **Stovetop candied yams.** Sliced sweet potatoes simmered in butter, brown sugar and a pinch of cinnamon until glossy and tender.
5. **Baked candied yams.** The same idea in a covered dish in the oven, basted once or twice so the syrup thickens.
6. **Southern sweet potato pie.** Silky, spiced and a little lighter than pumpkin pie. It can sit on the side dish table or the dessert table.

## What are some savory sweet potato recipes for Thanksgiving?

Not everyone wants dessert next to the turkey. Savory sweet potatoes balance a plate full of rich, sweet sides.

7. **Garlic and herb mashed sweet potatoes.** Roasted garlic, butter, a splash of milk, salt and fresh thyme or sage.
8. **Chipotle mashed sweet potatoes.** A spoonful of chipotle in adobo adds smoke and gentle heat.
9. **Roasted sweet potato cubes with rosemary.** Olive oil, salt, pepper and chopped rosemary at high heat until the edges brown.
10. **Sweet potato and caramelized onion gratin.** Thin slices layered with cream, onions and cheese, baked until bubbly.
11. **Brown butter sage sweet potatoes.** Roasted pieces tossed in nutty brown butter with crispy sage leaves.
12. **Sweet potato and Brussels sprouts sheet pan.** Two sides, one pan. Pair it with ideas from our [Thanksgiving Brussels sprouts roundup](../thanksgiving-recipes-brussel-sprouts/).
13. **Loaded sweet potato skins.** Twice-baked halves with cheese, scallions and a little bacon. Good as an appetizer too.

## What are some lighter or newer ideas?

If your table already has two casseroles, try something brighter.

14. **Hot honey roasted wedges.** Roasted until crisp, then drizzled with honey and a pinch of chili flakes.
15. **Hasselback sweet potatoes.** Thin cuts most of the way through, brushed with butter and spices so they fan open in the oven.
16. **Miso maple glazed sweet potatoes.** Salty, sweet and deeply savory.
17. **Sweet potato salad with pecans and cranberries.** Roasted cubes, toasted nuts, dried cranberries and a mustard vinaigrette. Serve warm or at room temperature.
18. **Coconut mashed sweet potatoes.** Coconut milk instead of dairy, with a little ginger. Naturally dairy-free.
19. **Smashed sweet potatoes with feta and herbs.** Boil small ones, smash them flat, roast until crisp and finish with crumbled feta.
20. **Sweet potato rolls.** Mashed sweet potato in the dough makes soft, golden dinner rolls.

## Which sweet potato recipes free up the oven?

On Thanksgiving, oven space is the real bottleneck. These keep the potatoes out of the way.

21. **Slow cooker candied yams.** Low and slow for a few hours, then held on warm. See our [crockpot Thanksgiving recipes](../thanksgiving-recipes-in-crockpot/) for more dishes that work this way.
22. **Slow cooker mashed sweet potatoes.** Cubes cook in a little broth or water, then get mashed right in the pot.
23. **Stovetop mash.** Boil peeled chunks in salted water until fork-tender, about 15 to 20 minutes, then mash with butter.
24. **Microwave baked sweet potatoes.** Pierce, microwave until soft, then split and top. A quick backup when the oven is full.

## How do you make sweet potato sides ahead of time?

Most sweet potato dishes are forgiving, which makes them perfect for prepping early.

### Two to three days ahead

- **Bake or boil the potatoes.** Cool them, then refrigerate whole or mashed in a covered container.
- **Make the pecan streusel.** Store it in the fridge in a sealed bag.

### One day ahead

- **Assemble casseroles and gratins** without the topping. Cover and refrigerate.
- **Peel and cut raw sweet potatoes** and keep them submerged in cold water in the fridge so they do not dry out.

### On the day

- Bring casseroles out of the fridge about 30 minutes before baking. Add toppings, then bake until hot in the center, adding 10 to 15 minutes to the time since the dish starts cold.
- Add marshmallows near the end and watch them closely. They go from golden to burnt fast.
- Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), and get them into the fridge within 2 hours of serving.

## How many sweet potatoes do you need for Thanksgiving?

A simple rule: about half a pound (225 g) of raw sweet potato per person. One medium sweet potato weighs roughly that much.

| Guests | Sweet potatoes (raw) | Roughly how many medium |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2 lb (0.9 kg) | 4 |
| 6 | 3 lb (1.4 kg) | 6 |
| 8 | 4 lb (1.8 kg) | 8 |
| 10 | 5 lb (2.3 kg) | 10 |
| 12 | 6 lb (2.7 kg) | 12 |

If you are serving sweet potatoes and mashed potatoes, you can trim this by about a quarter. To resize a recipe for your crowd, try the [recipe scaler](../../tools/recipe-scaler/).

## What goes well with sweet potato casserole?

Sweet potatoes are sweet and soft, so pair them with sides that are sharp, green or crunchy:

- Green beans or Brussels sprouts for something fresh and a little bitter.
- Cranberry sauce for tartness.
- A crisp salad with a vinegar dressing.
- Savory stuffing or dressing with herbs.

If your table leans sweet already, pick a savory option from the list above instead of a second marshmallow dish.

## Can you swap ingredients in sweet potato recipes?

Yes. Sweet potato dishes are easy to adapt.

- **Dairy-free:** Use olive oil or a plant butter, and coconut milk instead of cream.
- **Less sugar:** Cut the brown sugar in half. Roasting brings out plenty of natural sweetness on its own.
- **No maple syrup?** Honey or brown sugar works. Our [maple syrup substitute page](../../tools/substitute/maple-syrup/) has ratios.
- **Nut-free:** Swap the pecan streusel for an oat and brown sugar crumble.
