The best Thanksgiving recipes casserole cooks rely on are the ones you can build ahead and bake while the turkey rests. Think green bean casserole, sweet potato bake, cornbread stuffing, creamy corn, and baked mac and cheese. Below are 24 casserole ideas grouped by type, plus a simple plan for prepping, scaling and reheating them so the big day stays calm.

Casseroles are the quiet heroes of the holiday table. They travel well, feed a crowd, and free up your stovetop when you need it most.

## Why are casseroles perfect for Thanksgiving?

A casserole does three jobs at once. It feeds a lot of people from one dish. It can be made a day or two early. And it reheats well, which matters when the oven is busy with a turkey for hours.

They're also easy to share. If guests ask what to bring, a casserole in a 9x13-inch dish is the perfect answer.

## What are the classic Thanksgiving casseroles?

These are the dishes most families expect to see. Each line describes the general idea, so you can use your own favorite recipe.

### The must-haves

1. **Green bean casserole.** Tender green beans in a creamy mushroom sauce, topped with crispy fried onions.
2. **Sweet potato casserole.** Mashed sweet potatoes with warm spices, under marshmallows or a pecan crumble.
3. **Cornbread stuffing bake.** Crumbled cornbread with celery, onion, sage and broth, baked until golden on top.
4. **Classic bread stuffing.** Cubed bread, herbs and butter, baked in a dish instead of the bird.
5. **Baked mac and cheese.** Creamy cheese sauce and pasta with a browned, bubbly top.
6. **Creamy corn casserole.** A soft, spoonable corn pudding that sits somewhere between cornbread and custard.

## How do you make Thanksgiving casseroles ahead of time?

A little planning makes the day much easier.

### A simple prep timeline

- **2 to 3 days before:** Shop, make cornbread for stuffing, and cube or dry the bread.
- **1 to 2 days before:** Assemble casseroles in their dishes, cover tightly, and refrigerate. Keep toppings in separate containers.
- **Thanksgiving morning:** Take casseroles out of the fridge about 30 minutes before baking so the dish isn't ice-cold going into the oven.
- **While the turkey rests:** Add toppings and bake. Cold casseroles usually need 10 to 20 extra minutes.

Use oven-safe dishes and don't move a glass dish straight from the fridge into a very hot oven, since sudden temperature changes can crack it.

## How do you scale casseroles for a crowd?

A standard 9x13-inch dish serves about 10 to 12 people as a side. An 8x8-inch dish serves 6 to 8. If your recipe is written for a smaller dish and you're feeding 16, double it and use two pans instead of one deep one. Deep casseroles cook unevenly and take much longer in the middle.

## How do you keep casseroles safe on Thanksgiving?

Food safety matters most when dishes sit out for a long meal.

- Don't leave cooked casseroles at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
- Refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers so they cool quickly.
- Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C) in the center.
- Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days, or freeze them.
- Bake stuffing in a dish to 165°F in the center for food safety.
