The best Thanksgiving green beans are the ones that fit your oven and your schedule. Roasted beans are quick and crisp. Casserole is nostalgic and can mostly be built ahead. A stovetop sauté takes ten minutes and frees the oven. Below are 12 ideas, grouped by how much oven space they need, with the prep you can do days early.

These are general ideas and techniques, so you can adjust them to your own family recipe. If you are still planning the whole spread, start with our [Thanksgiving menu and cooking timeline](../thanksgiving-recipes-menu/).

## Which green bean dish fits your Thanksgiving?

Pick by oven space first. It is the thing everyone runs out of.

| Style | Oven needed? | Make ahead | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic casserole | Yes, 350°F (175°C) | Assemble day before | Traditional tables |
| Roasted with garlic | Yes, 425°F (220°C) | Trim and blanch early | Crisp, simple |
| Garlic butter sauté | No | Blanch early | Tight oven days |
| Almondine | No | Blanch early | Fancy and fast |

## What are the classic green bean casserole ideas?

### 1. The traditional casserole

Green beans, a creamy mushroom sauce and crispy fried onions on top. Baked until bubbling. It is the dish many people expect, so it is hard to skip.

### 2. From-scratch casserole

Swap canned soup for a sauce of sautéed mushrooms, butter, flour and milk or cream. It takes about 15 more minutes and tastes fresher.

### 3. Cheesy casserole

Stir a handful of shredded Gruyère or sharp cheddar into the sauce. Keep the onions on top for crunch.

### 4. Casserole with a toasted crumb topping

Use buttered breadcrumbs, or panko with a little garlic, in place of fried onions. It is easier to make from the pantry.

## What are the roasted and quick oven options?

### 5. Garlic roasted green beans

Toss trimmed beans with oil, salt and sliced garlic. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for about 12 to 15 minutes, until blistered at the tips. Add garlic partway through so it does not burn.

### 6. Parmesan roasted green beans

Same idea, with grated Parmesan scattered on for the last 3 to 4 minutes.

### 7. Balsamic and shallot roasted beans

Roast with thin shallot slices. Finish with a small splash of balsamic vinegar after they come out.

## What can you cook on the stovetop?

When the oven is full of turkey and stuffing, the stovetop is your friend. For help with the other oven dishes, see our [Thanksgiving dressing and stuffing ideas](../dressing-for-thanksgiving-recipes/).

### 8. Garlic butter sautéed beans

Blanch the beans, then toss them in a hot pan with butter and garlic for 2 to 3 minutes. Season and serve.

### 9. Green beans almondine

Brown butter in a pan, add sliced almonds until golden, then toss in the blanched beans with a squeeze of lemon.

### 10. Green beans with bacon

Cook chopped bacon until crisp, then warm the blanched beans in a little of the fat. Add a pinch of pepper.

### 11. Lemon, herb and feta beans

A lighter option. Dress the beans with lemon juice, olive oil, soft herbs and crumbled feta. These are good served warm or at room temperature.

### 12. Slow-cooker braised green beans

Cook beans slowly with onion, a little stock and smoked meat or smoked paprika until very tender. This frees up both the stovetop and the oven.

## How do you make green beans ahead for Thanksgiving?

Here is a simple plan.

- **2 days before:** trim the beans. Blanch them in salted boiling water for 2 to 4 minutes. Chill in ice water, dry them very well and refrigerate in a container lined with paper towel.
- **1 day before:** build casserole sauce. Assemble the casserole without the topping and cover it.
- **Thanksgiving day:** bake or sauté. Add crispy onions or crumbs near the end so they stay crisp.

Always cool cooked dishes quickly and keep them at or below 40°F (4°C) in the fridge. Reheat leftovers until steaming hot, to 165°F (74°C).

## What are the best tips for great green beans?

- **Salt the water.** Blanching water should taste like the sea.
- **Dry the beans.** Wet beans steam instead of browning.
- **Do not crowd the pan.** Use two sheet pans if needed.
- **Add acid last.** Lemon or vinegar at the end keeps the color bright.
- **Frozen is fine.** For casseroles, cook from frozen and skip blanching.
