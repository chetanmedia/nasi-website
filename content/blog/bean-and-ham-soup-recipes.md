Ham and bean soup is one of the easiest, cheapest and most comforting soups you can make: simmer white beans with a ham bone or diced ham, onion, carrot, celery and broth until the beans turn creamy. Below are 18 ham and bean soup ideas for the stovetop, slow cooker and pressure cooker, plus tips for using up holiday ham and getting the texture just right.

## What goes into a classic ham and bean soup?

Most versions share the same simple base.

### The building blocks

- **Beans.** Dried navy or great northern beans are classic. Canned beans work for a quick version.
- **Ham.** A meaty leftover ham bone, a smoked ham hock, or diced cooked ham.
- **Aromatics.** Onion, carrot, celery and garlic.
- **Liquid.** Water or low-sodium broth. Ham is salty, so taste before adding more salt.
- **Seasoning.** Bay leaf, black pepper and thyme. A splash of vinegar at the end brightens it.

### The basic method

Soften the vegetables in a little oil, add the beans, ham and liquid, then simmer gently until the beans are tender. Pull out the bone, shred any meat back into the pot, and season.

## What are the best classic ham and bean soup recipes?

These are the cozy, old-school versions most families grew up with.

1. **Navy bean and ham bone soup.** The classic. Slow simmered until thick and creamy.
2. **Great northern bean soup.** Slightly larger beans that hold their shape a bit more.
3. **Southern ham and pinto beans.** Served with cornbread and a little hot sauce.
4. **Senate-style bean soup.** A famous simple version with navy beans, ham and onion, often mashed a little for body.
5. **15-bean soup with ham.** A colorful mix with lots of texture.
6. **Split pea and ham soup.** Technically a legume cousin, but it belongs on every ham soup list.

## What are easy slow cooker and Instant Pot versions?

Hands-off cooking is perfect for beans.

7. **Slow cooker ham and bean soup.** Everything in the pot in the morning, dinner by evening.
8. **Instant Pot ham and bean soup.** Dried beans, no soaking, done in about an hour with pressure and release time.
9. **Slow cooker ham hock and white beans.** Smoky and rich, great for a weekend.
10. **Pressure cooker black bean and ham soup.** Add cumin and a squeeze of lime.

A safety tip for mixes: dried red kidney beans contain a natural toxin that a slow cooker may not destroy. If your mix includes them, boil the beans hard on the stovetop for 10 minutes before they go into the slow cooker.

## How do I use a leftover ham bone?

After a holiday ham, the bone is gold. Wrap it and refrigerate for up to 3 to 4 days, or freeze it for later.

### Turning it into soup

- Put the bone in the pot with the beans and cover with water or broth.
- Simmer gently, partly covered, until the beans are tender and the meat falls off the bone.
- Lift out the bone, let it cool slightly, and pull off the meat.
- Skim excess fat from the top if you like a lighter soup.

## How do I get perfectly creamy beans?

A few small habits make a big difference.

### Tips that help

- **Simmer, don't boil hard.** A hard boil breaks beans apart and toughens skins.
- **Add acidic ingredients late.** Tomatoes and vinegar can slow down softening, so add them once the beans are tender.
- **Use fresh dried beans.** Very old beans can take much longer to soften.
- **Mash a few.** Press a ladle of beans against the side of the pot for a thicker soup.
- **Taste at the end.** Ham adds plenty of salt, so season last.

### Storing and reheating

Cool the soup within 2 hours, then refrigerate for 3 to 4 days or freeze for 2 to 3 months. It thickens in the fridge, so add a splash of water or broth when you reheat. Bring leftovers to a steaming simmer before serving.
