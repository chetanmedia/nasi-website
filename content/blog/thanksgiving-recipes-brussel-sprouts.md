The best Thanksgiving recipes with Brussels sprouts cook them hot and fast so the edges caramelize: roasted with bacon, glazed with maple or balsamic, seared in a skillet, or shaved raw into a crunchy salad. Done this way, they taste nutty and sweet, not bitter. Below are 22 ideas sorted by cooking method, plus how much to buy and how to fit them around the turkey.

## Why are Brussels sprouts good for Thanksgiving?

They are in season in late fall, they are cheap, and they bring something green and crisp to a table full of soft, rich dishes. A plate of mashed potatoes, stuffing and gravy needs something with a little bite.

They also adapt to almost any flavor. Sweet, salty, tangy, cheesy: Brussels sprouts take them all.

## How do you cook Brussels sprouts so they are not bitter?

Most bad memories of Brussels sprouts come from boiling them too long. Here is how to get them right.

### Pick the right sprouts

Look for small to medium sprouts that are firm, tight and bright green. Skip any with yellow leaves or soft spots. Similar sizes cook evenly.

### Prep them well

Trim the dry stem end, pull off any loose or yellow outer leaves, and cut them in half through the stem. Halves get a flat side that browns beautifully.

### Use high heat

Roast at 425°F (220°C), cut side down, until the bottoms are deep brown. Do not crowd the pan, or they steam instead of roasting. Use two sheet pans if you need to.

### Season at the end

A splash of acid (lemon, balsamic, vinegar) and something sweet (maple, honey, a bit of cranberry) right after cooking makes them taste brighter.

## What are the best roasted Brussels sprouts ideas for Thanksgiving?

Roasting is the classic. These all start the same way: halved sprouts, oil, salt and pepper on a hot sheet pan.

### Roasted ideas

1. **Bacon and maple.** Toss with crisp bacon pieces and a drizzle of maple syrup at the end.
2. **Balsamic glaze.** Drizzle with reduced balsamic just before serving.
3. **Parmesan crusted.** Roast cut side down on grated Parmesan so a crispy cheese layer forms.
4. **Cranberry and pecan.** Toss with dried cranberries and toasted pecans for a festive mix.
5. **Garlic and lemon.** Add sliced garlic for the last 10 minutes, finish with lemon zest.
6. **Hot honey.** A drizzle of hot honey for a sweet and spicy kick.
7. **With butternut squash.** Roast cubes of squash on the same pan for color and sweetness.
8. **With pomegranate.** Scatter pomegranate seeds on top for crunch and sparkle.
9. **Miso butter.** Toss hot sprouts with butter mixed with a little miso.

## Can you make Brussels sprouts on the stove?

Yes, and on Thanksgiving it is often the smarter move, since the oven is full.

### Stovetop ideas

10. **Skillet seared.** Halved sprouts cut side down in a hot skillet with oil until charred, then a splash of water and a lid to finish.
11. **Brown butter and sage.** Sear, then finish with browned butter and crisped sage leaves.
12. **Shredded and sautéed.** Thinly sliced sprouts cooked quickly with shallots and butter.
13. **With pancetta and chestnuts.** A rich, classic holiday pairing.
14. **Sweet and sour.** Sear, then glaze with a mix of soy sauce, vinegar and a little honey.
15. **Creamed Brussels sprouts.** Shredded sprouts simmered briefly with cream and nutmeg until tender.

## What are some Brussels sprouts salads and casseroles?

Not every dish needs to be roasted. These add variety to the table.

### Raw and shaved

16. **Shaved Brussels sprouts salad.** Thinly sliced raw sprouts, lemon, olive oil, Parmesan and toasted almonds. Make it 30 minutes ahead so the leaves soften slightly.
17. **Apple and cheddar slaw.** Shaved sprouts with crisp apple, sharp cheddar and a mustard dressing.
18. **Harvest salad.** Shaved sprouts with kale, dried cranberries, pumpkin seeds and a maple vinaigrette.

### Baked and casseroles

19. **Brussels sprouts gratin.** Halved sprouts baked in a creamy cheese sauce under a crumb topping.
20. **Mac and cheese with sprouts.** Fold roasted sprouts into baked mac and cheese.
21. **Brussels sprouts and potato hash.** Crispy potatoes and sprouts with onions, great for using leftovers on Friday.
22. **Sheet pan with sausage.** Roasted sprouts with sliced sausage and apples, a hearty option for smaller gatherings.

### Make-ahead plan

- **Two days before:** Trim and halve. Store dry in the fridge.
- **Thanksgiving morning:** Make any dressings, toast nuts, cook bacon.
- **While the turkey rests:** Turn the oven up to 425°F (220°C) and roast the sprouts. The turkey needs 20 to 30 minutes of rest, which is exactly how long the sprouts take.
- **Right before serving:** Toss with glaze, cheese or toppings.

If your oven will be taken until the last minute, choose a stovetop version or the shaved salad. One green vegetable side is usually enough, two if you have a big crowd. If you are serving green bean casserole too, pick a lighter, tangy sprout recipe, like the lemon garlic or shaved salad, so the table is not all creamy.
