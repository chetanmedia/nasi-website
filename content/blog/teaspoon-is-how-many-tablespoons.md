A teaspoon is one third of a tablespoon. Put another way, **3 teaspoons equal 1 tablespoon**. That is true for every US recipe, and for UK ones too. Keep that one fact in your head and you can work out almost any other swap.

Below is a chart for the amounts you will actually meet, then a few tricks for odd amounts.

## What is the quick teaspoon to tablespoon chart?

Use level spoons for all of these. Metric amounts are in brackets.

| Teaspoons (tsp) | Tablespoons (tbsp) | Milliliters (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1/4 tsp | 1/12 tbsp | 1.25 mL |
| 1/2 tsp | 1/6 tbsp | 2.5 mL |
| 1 tsp | 1/3 tbsp | 5 mL |
| 1 1/2 tsp | 1/2 tbsp | 7.5 mL |
| 2 tsp | 2/3 tbsp | 10 mL |
| 3 tsp | 1 tbsp | 15 mL |
| 4 tsp | 1 1/3 tbsp | 20 mL |
| 6 tsp | 2 tbsp (1 fluid ounce) | 30 mL |
| 9 tsp | 3 tbsp | 45 mL |
| 12 tsp | 4 tbsp (1/4 cup) | 60 mL |
| 48 tsp | 16 tbsp (1 cup) | 240 mL |

## How many tablespoons is 1 teaspoon?

One teaspoon is 1/3 of a tablespoon. You cannot measure a third of a tablespoon on most spoons, so just use the teaspoon. That is the whole point of having one.

The other direction is easier. Want to use a tablespoon instead of a teaspoon? Divide the teaspoons by 3.

- 6 tsp ÷ 3 = 2 tbsp
- 7 tsp = 2 tbsp + 1 tsp
- 10 tsp = 3 tbsp + 1 tsp

## How do you convert teaspoons to tablespoons?

Divide by 3. That is the entire rule.

- **Teaspoons to tablespoons:** divide by 3. 12 tsp ÷ 3 = 4 tbsp.
- **Tablespoons to teaspoons:** multiply by 3. 2 tbsp × 3 = 6 tsp.

When the answer is not a whole number, split it. 5 tsp is 1 tbsp plus 2 tsp. Fractions of a tablespoon are rarely worth measuring. Switch back to teaspoons and keep going.

### What about half and quarter amounts?

Recipes love these.

- Half a tablespoon is 1 1/2 teaspoons.
- A quarter of a tablespoon is 3/4 teaspoon. Measure 1/2 tsp plus 1/4 tsp.
- Three quarters of a tablespoon is 2 1/4 teaspoons.

### Are dry and liquid ingredients the same?

The volume is the same. A tablespoon of olive oil and a tablespoon of flour both fill the same spoon, and both are 3 teaspoons. What differs is weight. A level tablespoon of butter is about 14 grams, while a level tablespoon of table salt is much heavier. If a recipe lists grams, trust the grams.

## Does the type of spoon matter?

Yes, a little. Use real measuring spoons, not the spoons from your cutlery drawer. Those vary a lot in size.

Two more things to know:

- **Level, not heaped.** Scrape the top flat with a knife. A heaped teaspoon can hold nearly twice as much, which matters for baking powder, baking soda and salt.
- **Check where the recipe comes from.** US and UK tablespoons are 15 mL. An Australian tablespoon is 20 mL, which is 4 teaspoons. If a recipe uses an Australian tablespoon, your 3-teaspoon rule will leave you short.

## Which common recipe amounts are worth memorizing?

A few that come up all the time:

- 3 tsp = 1 tbsp
- 2 tbsp = 6 tsp = 1 fluid ounce
- 4 tbsp = 12 tsp = 1/4 cup
- 5 tbsp + 1 tsp = 1/3 cup
- 8 tbsp = 24 tsp = 1/2 cup

## How do you scale a recipe with teaspoons and tablespoons?

Halving or doubling is where the 3-to-1 rule saves you.

**Doubling:** 2 tsp doubled is 4 tsp, which is 1 tbsp + 1 tsp. 2 tbsp doubled is 4 tbsp, or 1/4 cup.

**Halving:** 1 tbsp halved is 1 1/2 tsp. 1 tsp halved is 1/2 tsp. 1/2 tsp halved is 1/4 tsp.

A tip for tiny amounts: when you halve 1/4 tsp, use 1/8 tsp, which is half of your smallest spoon. Do not eyeball baking powder or salt.
