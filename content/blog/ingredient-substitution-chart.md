Halfway through a recipe and missing one ingredient? Before you run to the store, check this chart. These are the swaps that work, with the ratios that matter.

## Quick substitution chart

| If you are out of | Use this instead | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 cup buttermilk | 1 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + milk to make 1 cup, rest 5–10 min | Pancakes, biscuits, marinades |
| 1 cup heavy cream (cooking) | 3/4 cup whole milk + 1/4 cup melted butter | Sauces, soups (not for whipping) |
| 1 cup half and half | 1/2 cup whole milk + 1/2 cup heavy cream | Coffee, sauces |
| 1 cup sour cream | 1 cup plain Greek yogurt | Dips, dressings, baking |
| 1 egg (baking) | 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water, rest 5 min | Muffins, cookies, quick breads |
| 1 egg (baking) | 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce | Cakes, brownies |
| 1 tsp baking powder | 1/4 tsp baking soda + 1/2 tsp cream of tartar | Any baking |
| 1 cup self-rising flour | 1 cup all-purpose flour + 1 1/2 tsp baking powder + 1/4 tsp salt | Biscuits, quick breads |
| 1 cup cake flour | 1 cup all-purpose flour minus 2 tbsp, plus 2 tbsp cornstarch | Cakes |
| 1 cup brown sugar | 1 cup white sugar + 1 tbsp molasses | Cookies, sauces |
| 1 tbsp cornstarch (thickening) | 2 tbsp all-purpose flour | Gravies, stews |
| 1 tbsp fresh herbs | 1 tsp dried herbs | Everything |
| 1 garlic clove | 1/8 tsp garlic powder | Rubs, sauces |
| 1 tbsp lemon juice | 1 tbsp lime juice, or 1/2 tbsp vinegar | Dressings, marinades |
| 1/2 cup white wine | 1/2 cup chicken or vegetable stock + 1 tsp vinegar | Pan sauces, risotto |
| 1 cup honey | 1 cup maple syrup | Glazes, dressings |

## Dairy swaps

**Buttermilk** is the swap people search for most. The lemon-or-vinegar trick works because the acid thickens the milk slightly and reacts with baking soda the same way real buttermilk does.

**Heavy cream** for cooking can be replaced with milk and melted butter, which gets you close to the fat content. It will not whip, so for whipped cream there is no shortcut.

**Sour cream and Greek yogurt** swap one for one. Full-fat yogurt gives the closest result.

## Egg swaps for baking

Eggs bind, lift and add moisture. A **flax egg** (1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water) is best for binding in cookies and muffins. **Applesauce** adds moisture and works in cakes and brownies. For recipes with three or more eggs, substitutes struggle, so pick a different recipe.

## Leavening and flour swaps

Baking powder is baking soda plus an acid, so you can make it from baking soda and **cream of tartar**. Do not swap baking soda for baking powder one for one: it is about three to four times stronger and needs an acid to work.

## Flavor swaps

Fresh herbs to dried is roughly **3 to 1**. Add dried herbs early in cooking so they soften. Add fresh herbs at the end.
