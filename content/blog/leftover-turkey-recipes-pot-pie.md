The easiest leftover turkey recipes for pot pie and casseroles start with cooked turkey, a creamy sauce, and vegetables, baked under a crust, biscuits or a crunchy topping until bubbling. Because the turkey is already cooked, most of these dishes take 45 minutes to an hour. Below is a base pot pie method you can riff on, 16 casserole ideas sorted by mood, and the safety basics so the leftovers stay good.

One rule first: get the turkey into the fridge within 2 hours of serving, and use it within 3 to 4 days.

## How do you make turkey pot pie with leftover turkey?

This base serves 6 and fits a deep 9-inch pie dish or a 2-quart baking dish.

1. **Make the filling.** Melt 4 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook 1 diced onion, 2 diced carrots and 2 sliced celery ribs until soft, about 8 minutes.
2. **Thicken it.** Stir in 1/3 cup of flour and cook for a minute. Slowly whisk in 2 cups of turkey or chicken broth and 1 cup of milk. Simmer until thick, about 5 minutes.
3. **Add the turkey.** Stir in 3 to 4 cups of chopped cooked turkey, 1 cup of frozen peas, and a pinch of thyme. Season with salt and pepper.
4. **Top it.** Pour the filling into the dish. Cover with a pie crust, cut a few vents, and brush with beaten egg.
5. **Bake.** Bake at 400°F for 30 to 35 minutes, until the crust is golden and the filling bubbles at the edges. Let it rest 10 minutes before serving.

### Shortcuts that still taste homemade

- **Use leftover gravy.** Swap some of the broth for gravy. It thickens and seasons the filling at once.
- **Use frozen mixed vegetables.** No chopping, and they hold up well in the oven.
- **Skip the bottom crust.** A top-only pot pie is faster and never gets soggy.

## What can you top a turkey pot pie with?

The topping changes the whole dish. Pick by how much time you have.

| Topping | Time to prep | Texture | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store-bought pie crust | 5 minutes | Flaky, classic | Vent it so steam escapes |
| Puff pastry | 5 minutes | Light, very crisp | Bake on a sheet to catch drips |
| Biscuits (canned or homemade) | 5 to 15 minutes | Soft, fluffy | Leave gaps so they cook through |
| Leftover stuffing | 2 minutes | Crunchy, savory | Drizzle with butter or broth first |
| Mashed potatoes | 2 minutes | Creamy, shepherd's pie style | Rough up the top so it browns |

## What are the best leftover turkey casserole ideas?

Here are 16 ideas grouped by mood. Each is a general idea, so use your favorite version or adapt the pot pie base above.

### Cozy and classic

1. **Turkey and stuffing bake.** Turkey and gravy on the bottom, leftover stuffing on top.
2. **Turkey tetrazzini.** Spaghetti, turkey and mushrooms in a creamy sauce with a Parmesan top.
3. **Turkey and wild rice casserole.** Cooked wild rice, mushrooms and turkey in a light cream sauce.
4. **Turkey shepherd's pie.** Turkey and vegetables in gravy under a layer of leftover mashed potatoes.

### Quick weeknight bakes

5. **Turkey noodle casserole.** Egg noodles, peas and turkey with a crunchy breadcrumb topping.
6. **Turkey and broccoli rice bake.** Rice, broccoli and turkey with melted cheddar.
7. **Biscuit-topped turkey skillet.** The pot pie filling in a cast iron skillet with biscuits on top.
8. **Turkey mac and cheese.** Stir chopped turkey into baked mac and cheese before it goes in the oven.

### A little spicy

9. **Turkey enchilada casserole.** Layer tortillas, turkey, beans, enchilada sauce and cheese.
10. **Turkey tamale pie.** Spiced turkey filling under a cornbread topping.
11. **Buffalo turkey bake.** Turkey, pasta and a creamy buffalo sauce with blue cheese or ranch on the side.
12. **Green chile turkey and rice.** Rice, green chiles, turkey and pepper jack.

### Make ahead and freeze

13. **Mini turkey pot pies.** Bake the filling in ramekins or muffin tins for easy single portions.
14. **Turkey lasagna.** White sauce, spinach and turkey layered with noodles.
15. **Turkey and sweet potato bake.** Use up leftover sweet potatoes as the top layer.
16. **Turkey breakfast strata.** Bread, eggs, milk, turkey and cheese, assembled the night before.

## How do you store and reheat turkey casseroles safely?

- **Assemble cold, bake hot.** If you build a casserole ahead, keep it covered in the fridge and bake it within a day.
- **Fridge.** Baked casseroles keep for 3 to 4 days, but count from when the turkey was first cooked. Day-four turkey shouldn't become a new casserole that lasts another four days.
- **Freezer.** Freeze unbaked or baked casseroles, well wrapped, and use within about 3 months for the best quality.
- **Reheat to 165°F.** Cover the dish with foil so it doesn't dry out, and check the center with a thermometer.
