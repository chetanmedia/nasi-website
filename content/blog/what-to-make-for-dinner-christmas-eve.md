The best thing to make for dinner on Christmas Eve is something warm, easy and mostly made ahead: a pot of soup or stew, a baked pasta, a seafood dinner, or a grazing board everyone picks at. Christmas Day already has a big meal planned, so Christmas Eve is your chance to cook something cozy without stress.

Below are 24 ideas grouped by mood and by how much time you have, plus a simple plan so the two days do not crash into each other.

## How do you choose a Christmas Eve dinner?

Start with three questions. They narrow the list fast.

### How much time will you have?

If you are wrapping presents, traveling or picking people up, pick a dish that is made ahead or cooks itself in a slow cooker. If the afternoon is free, you can cook something more hands-on.

### What is happening on Christmas Day?

If you are roasting a turkey or ham tomorrow, keep the oven free on the 24th and lean on the stove. Skip anything heavy that competes with tomorrow's menu. If tomorrow is casual, Christmas Eve can be the showpiece. Our [Christmas dinner ideas](../christmas-dinner-ideas/) can help you balance the two.

### Who is coming?

Kids, picky eaters and big groups all point toward build-your-own meals. A quiet night for two can be something a little fancier.

## What are cozy Christmas Eve dinner ideas?

These are warm, comforting and easy to keep on the stove while people arrive.

### Soups and stews

1. **Creamy potato and leek soup** with crusty bread and butter.
2. **Beef and vegetable stew**, slow-simmered and even better the next day.
3. **Italian wedding soup** with tiny meatballs and greens. See our [Italian wedding soup guide](../soup-recipes-italian-wedding/).
4. **White chicken chili** with lime, cilantro and tortilla chips on the side.
5. **French onion soup** with toasted cheese bread on top.
6. **Seafood chowder** with corn, potatoes and whatever fish looks good.

### Bakes and casseroles

7. **Classic lasagna**, assembled the day before and baked in the evening.
8. **Baked ziti** with sausage or roasted vegetables.
9. **Chicken pot pie** with a store-bought crust to save time.
10. **Enchiladas** in red or green sauce, made ahead and baked to bubbling.

## What are festive but easy Christmas Eve dinners?

These feel a little special without a full day of cooking.

### Seafood nights

11. **Garlic butter shrimp pasta**, ready in about 20 minutes.
12. **Roasted salmon** with lemon, herbs and a big green salad.
13. **Mussels in white wine and garlic** with bread for dipping.
14. **Baked cod** with a buttery breadcrumb topping and roast potatoes.

### Small roasts and showpieces

15. **Roast chicken** with root vegetables, if Christmas Day is ham or beef.
16. **Pork tenderloin** with a mustard or herb crust.
17. **Stuffed shells** in tomato sauce with ricotta and spinach.
18. **Mushroom and spinach wellington** for a vegetarian centerpiece.

## What are fun Christmas Eve dinners for families?

Build-your-own meals keep everyone happy and let kids help.

### Make-your-own nights

19. **Taco or burrito bar** with a few fillings and lots of toppings.
20. **Homemade pizza night** with dough balls and everyone choosing toppings.
21. **Baked potato bar** with chili, cheese, sour cream and greens.
22. **Fondue night** with cheese for bread and vegetables, and chocolate for fruit.

### Grazing and snacking

23. **Christmas grazing board** with cheese, cured meats, crackers, fruit and dips.
24. **Appetizer dinner**: a few warm bites like meatballs, sliders and stuffed mushrooms, eaten in front of a movie.

## How do you plan Christmas Eve and Christmas Day together?

Treat the two days as one menu. It saves money, time and fridge space.

### Shop once

Write both menus side by side, then build one shopping list. Ingredients like herbs, cream, potatoes and bread often show up in both meals. Our guide to [planning a week of meals](../how-to-meal-plan-for-a-week/) uses the same idea.

### Share the work across days

- **December 22 or 23:** make the soup, sauce or casserole for Christmas Eve. Make any desserts that keep.
- **Christmas Eve morning:** prep vegetables for both days. Set the table for tomorrow.
- **Christmas Eve evening:** reheat dinner and enjoy it.
- **Christmas Day:** focus on the main event.

### Keep make-ahead food safe

Cool cooked food within 2 hours, and divide big batches into shallow containers so they chill fast. Keep them in the fridge at 40°F or below, and eat within 3 to 4 days. Reheat soups, stews and casseroles until they are steaming hot all the way through (165°F). If you are thawing anything frozen, do it in the fridge, not on the counter.

### Plan for leftovers

Christmas Eve leftovers make an easy lunch on the 26th, when nobody wants to cook. If you will have a crowd later in the week, our ideas for [Christmas dinner for large groups](../christmas-dinner-ideas-for-large-groups/) show how to stretch a menu.

## What sides and desserts go with Christmas Eve dinner?

Keep the extras simple. One side, one bread and one easy dessert is plenty when a bigger meal is coming.

### Easy sides

- **A big green salad** with a sharp vinaigrette cuts through rich soups and bakes.
- **Garlic bread or crusty rolls** for anything with a sauce or broth.
- **Roasted carrots or Brussels sprouts**, which can share the oven with a casserole.

If you want more choices, our list of [Christmas dinner sides](../christmas-dinner-ideas-sides/) works for both nights.

### Low-effort desserts

- **A plate of Christmas cookies**, baked earlier in the week.
- **Store-bought ice cream** with warm chocolate sauce.
- **Fruit and cheese** for anyone who wants something light.

Cookies are the classic for a reason. They keep for days and double as a gift. Our [easy Christmas cookies](../christmas-cookies-easy/) are a good place to start.

### Drinks

A pot of hot cocoa or mulled apple cider on low heat makes the house smell like the holidays, and lets guests help themselves. Keep a pitcher of water and something sparkling and alcohol-free on the table for kids and drivers.
