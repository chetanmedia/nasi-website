The best New Year's Eve dinner ideas are festive but low-stress: one standout main, like steak, roast salmon or a big baked pasta, a couple of easy sides, and something fun to nibble toward midnight. Below are 24 ideas grouped by the kind of night you're planning, plus a simple timeline so the cooking is done well before the countdown.

## What should I make for New Year's Eve dinner?

Start with your night, not the recipe. A cozy dinner for two needs a different plan than a crowd of friends grazing until midnight. Pick a group below and choose one main, one or two sides and a dessert.

### A quick way to build the menu

- **One special main.** This is where you spend your effort.
- **Two easy sides.** A salad and a starch are plenty.
- **One make-ahead dessert.** No baking at 11 PM.
- **Something to nibble later.** Midnight snacks keep the party going.

## What are elegant New Year's Eve dinner ideas?

These feel like a restaurant night but are very doable at home.

1. **Seared steak with garlic butter.** Ribeye or strip, rested well, with a spoonful of herby butter on top.
2. **Roast salmon with lemon and dill.** One big fillet on a sheet pan looks impressive and cooks in about 15 minutes.
3. **Pan-seared scallops.** A very hot pan, a dry scallop and two minutes per side.
4. **Beef tenderloin.** Roasted whole and sliced at the table. Use a thermometer and pull it a few degrees early.
5. **Mushroom risotto.** Creamy, cozy and a good vegetarian centerpiece.
6. **Shrimp scampi with linguine.** Garlic, butter, lemon and white wine, ready in 20 minutes.

## What are cozy New Year's Eve dinner ideas for a night in?

For pajamas, a movie and staying in where it's warm.

7. **Homemade pizza night.** Store-bought dough and everyone picks their toppings.
8. **Braised short ribs.** Low and slow all afternoon, so the house smells amazing.
9. **Chicken pot pie.** Golden crust, creamy filling, total comfort.
10. **French onion soup.** Bubbly cheese toast on top makes it feel like an occasion.
11. **Baked ziti or lasagna.** Assemble in the afternoon, bake before dinner.
12. **Fondue.** Cheese to start, chocolate for dessert, and it stretches a long evening.

## What are good New Year's Eve dinner ideas for a party?

For a crowd, think make-ahead and self-serve, so you can join the fun.

13. **Taco or nacho bar.** Cook the fillings ahead and set out toppings.
14. **Slow cooker meatballs.** Keep them warm on the counter all night.
15. **Sheet-pan chicken thighs.** Two pans feed a crowd with almost no effort.
16. **A big pot of chili.** Better made the day before. Set out cheese, sour cream and chips.
17. **Sliders.** Mini burgers or pulled pork on soft rolls.
18. **Grazing board.** Cheeses, cured meats, crackers, fruit, nuts and olives.

## What do people eat on New Year's Eve for good luck?

Lots of cultures have a lucky dish for the new year. Adding one is a lovely way to make the night feel meaningful.

19. **Black-eyed peas and greens.** A Southern US tradition, often with cornbread on the side.
20. **Lentils with sausage.** Popular in Italy, where the little lentils are said to look like coins.
21. **Long noodles.** In parts of Asia, long noodles stand for a long life. Try not to cut them.
22. **Grapes at midnight.** In Spain, people eat 12 grapes as the clock strikes, one for each month.
23. **Pork and sauerkraut.** A New Year's Day favorite in Pennsylvania and parts of Germany.
24. **Round foods.** Round fruits, ring-shaped cakes or donuts for a full circle of luck.

## How do I plan a New Year's Eve dinner timeline?

Working backward from when you want to eat keeps the night calm. Here's a simple plan for a 7:30 PM dinner.

### The day before

- Make dessert and any soup, chili or braise.
- Buy everything, including ice and drinks.
- Write a short prep list.

### The morning

- Set the table.
- Chop vegetables and make dressings or sauces.
- Assemble any baked pasta or casserole and refrigerate it.

### The evening

- **5:30 PM:** Take steak or other meat out of the fridge to lose some chill.
- **6:30 PM:** Start the oven and get bakes in.
- **7:00 PM:** Cook quick items like scallops, steak or shrimp.
- **7:30 PM:** Dinner.
- **11:00 PM:** Put out midnight snacks.

### A quick food safety note

On a long party night, don't leave perishable food out for more than 2 hours. Keep hot food hot in a slow cooker on warm, and keep cold dips over ice. Cook ground meat to 160°F, chicken to 165°F, and steaks and roasts to at least 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest.
