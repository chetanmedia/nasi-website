The best vegetable beef soup recipes start with well-browned chuck, a tomato and broth base, and whatever vegetables you have, simmered low until the beef falls apart. It takes about 2 hours of mostly hands-off time, or 40 minutes if you use ground beef. Below you'll find one simple base method, 20 twists grouped by time and mood, and the storage basics so a big pot lasts you all week.

This is the kind of soup that forgives you. Forget the celery? Fine. Only have frozen peas and half a bag of carrots? Also fine. Once you know the base, every version is just a swap.

## What goes into a classic vegetable beef soup?

Most versions share the same five building blocks. Get these right and the rest is personal taste.

### The beef

Chuck roast cut into 1-inch cubes is the classic. It has marbling and connective tissue that melt into the broth over a long simmer. Ground beef is the weeknight shortcut. Leftover pot roast is the lazy Sunday version, and it's excellent.

### The base

Beef broth plus a can of diced or crushed tomatoes. A spoonful of tomato paste, browned in the pot, adds depth. A splash of Worcestershire sauce brings the savory note people can never quite name.

### The aromatics

Onion, carrot and celery, cooked until soft. Garlic goes in for the last minute so it doesn't burn.

### The vegetables

Potatoes, green beans, corn and peas are the usual crew. Cabbage, zucchini, parsnips and turnips all work. Frozen mixed vegetables are a completely fair choice.

### The seasoning

Bay leaf, dried thyme, salt and black pepper. Some cooks add a pinch of oregano or smoked paprika. Taste at the end, since broth saltiness varies a lot by brand.

## How do you make vegetable beef soup? (the base method)

This method feeds 6 to 8 people. Treat it as a template, not a rulebook.

1. **Brown the beef.** Pat 2 pounds of cubed chuck dry and season it with salt and pepper. Brown it in batches in a little oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Crowding the pot steams the meat, so give it room. Move the browned beef to a plate.
2. **Soften the aromatics.** Lower the heat to medium. Add 1 diced onion, 2 diced carrots and 2 sliced celery ribs. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, scraping up the brown bits.
3. **Build the base.** Stir in 2 tablespoons of tomato paste and 3 minced garlic cloves for a minute. Add 8 cups of beef broth, a 14.5-ounce can of diced tomatoes, 1 tablespoon of Worcestershire, a bay leaf and 1 teaspoon of dried thyme.
4. **Simmer the beef.** Return the beef and its juices to the pot. Bring it to a boil, then drop it to a gentle simmer. Cover partly and cook for about 1 1/2 hours, until the beef is nearly tender.
5. **Add the vegetables.** Stir in 2 cups of cubed potatoes and 1 cup of green beans. Simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, until the potatoes are tender. Add 1 cup each of corn and peas for the last 5 minutes.
6. **Finish and taste.** Remove the bay leaf. Adjust salt and pepper. A squeeze of lemon or a splash of red wine vinegar brightens everything.

### Tips for tender beef

- **Keep it at a simmer, not a boil.** Small, lazy bubbles. A hard boil makes the meat tight and stringy.
- **Be patient through the tough stage.** Chuck firms up before it relaxes. If it's chewy at an hour, give it more time.
- **Don't skip the browning.** It's where most of the flavor comes from.

## What are the best vegetable beef soup variations?

Here are 20 ideas grouped by how much time you have and what you're in the mood for. Each one starts from the base above, with a few swaps.

### When you have 45 minutes or less

1. **Ground beef vegetable soup.** Brown 1 1/2 pounds of ground beef, drain the fat, and cut the simmer to 25 minutes.
2. **Frozen-vegetable shortcut.** Use a 16-ounce bag of frozen mixed vegetables in place of fresh ones and add it in the last 10 minutes.
3. **Leftover pot roast soup.** Shred cooked roast into the broth and simmer just until the vegetables are done.
4. **Hamburger soup with macaroni.** Add a cup of small pasta for the last 10 minutes and serve right away.
5. **Pressure cooker beef soup.** Brown the chuck on sauté mode, then pressure cook according to your appliance's manual and add quick-cooking vegetables after.

### When you want to set it and forget it

6. **Slow cooker vegetable beef soup.** Brown the beef first, then cook everything except peas and corn on low for 7 to 8 hours.
7. **Dutch oven in the oven.** Simmer the covered pot in a 325°F oven for about 2 hours. The steady heat keeps the beef tender.
8. **Big-batch freezer soup.** Double the recipe, skip the potatoes, and freeze in 2-cup portions. Add fresh potatoes when you reheat.
9. **Beef and barley soup.** Stir in 3/4 cup of pearl barley with the beef. It thickens the soup as it cooks.

### When you want something cozy and hearty

10. **Old-fashioned beef and potato soup.** Double the potatoes and skip the beans for a thicker, stew-like bowl.
11. **Beef, cabbage and potato soup.** Add 4 cups of shredded cabbage with the potatoes. Cheap, filling and great the next day.
12. **Creamy-style beef and vegetable soup.** Mash a few cooked potato cubes against the side of the pot to thicken the broth without cream.
13. **Beef and root vegetable soup.** Swap in parsnips, turnips and rutabaga for some of the potatoes.
14. **Beef and mushroom soup.** Brown a pound of sliced mushrooms after the beef. Add a sprig of rosemary.

### When you want a different flavor

15. **Italian-style beef minestrone.** Add zucchini, cannellini beans, oregano and a small pasta. Finish with Parmesan.
16. **Tex-Mex beef and vegetable soup.** Season with cumin and chili powder, add black beans and corn, and top with avocado.
17. **Beef and vegetable soup with red wine.** Deglaze the pot with a cup of dry red wine before adding the broth.
18. **Smoky paprika beef soup.** Add smoked paprika and a diced red bell pepper for a goulash-like feel.

### When you want it lighter

19. **Brothy beef and greens soup.** Use fewer potatoes and stir in chopped kale or spinach at the end.
20. **Low-carb beef soup.** Skip the potatoes and corn. Use cauliflower, green beans and zucchini instead.

## Should you use stew meat or ground beef?

Both make a great soup. They just suit different nights.

| | Cubed chuck or stew meat | Ground beef |
|---|---|---|
| Total time | About 2 hours | About 40 minutes |
| Texture | Tender, spoonable chunks | Small, crumbly bits |
| Flavor | Deep and beefy | Lighter, more like hamburger soup |
| Best for | Weekends, slow cookers, batch cooking | Busy weeknights |
| Cost | Moderate | Usually lower |

If you buy pre-cut stew meat, look at the pieces. Lean cuts like round can stay dry no matter how long you cook them. Cutting your own chuck roast gives you more even, tender results.

## How do you store and reheat vegetable beef soup safely?

A big pot of soup is a gift to future you, as long as you cool it properly.

- **Cool it fast.** Don't put a giant hot pot straight in the fridge. Divide the soup into shallow containers so it chills quickly, and refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking.
- **Fridge.** Covered, it keeps for 3 to 4 days.
- **Freezer.** Freeze in portions, leaving some room at the top for expansion. Use within 2 to 3 months for the best texture.
- **Reheat.** Warm only what you'll eat, until it's steaming hot all the way through. Thaw frozen soup in the fridge overnight or reheat it gently from frozen on the stove.
- **Pasta and rice.** They soak up broth and go mushy. Cook them separately and add to each bowl.

## What should you serve with vegetable beef soup?

The soup is a full meal on its own, but a few sides make it feel like dinner.

- Crusty bread or garlic toast for dunking
- Grilled cheese sandwiches, cut into strips
- Warm cornbread or biscuits
- A crisp green salad with a sharp vinaigrette
- Saltine crackers, the old-school way

## What if you only have a few of the ingredients?

That's the beauty of this soup. Beef, broth, an onion and any vegetable will make a good pot. Use what's in the crisper drawer, the freezer, or the back of the pantry. Canned beans, frozen spinach and that half bag of baby carrots all count.
