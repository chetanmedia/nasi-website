The easiest Christmas cookies for beginners are shortbread, drop cookies like chocolate chip or gingerbread crinkles, and no-bake treats such as peanut butter oat cookies. They use pantry ingredients, skip fussy rolling and decorating, and forgive small mistakes. Below are 24 easy Christmas cookie ideas grouped by how much time you have, plus the tips that make first batches turn out right.

## What makes a Christmas cookie easy?

Easy cookies have a short ingredient list, simple shaping and a wide margin for error. You want dough you can scoop or slice instead of roll and cut, and decorations that take a sprinkle rather than piping skills.

### Signs a recipe is beginner friendly

- Uses butter, sugar, flour, eggs and pantry staples.
- Scoop, roll into balls, or slice-and-bake instead of cutting shapes.
- Short chill time, or none.
- Simple finish: powdered sugar, sprinkles or a drizzle.

## What are easy Christmas cookies you can make in under 30 minutes?

These are quick drop and no-bake ideas for busy weeknights.

### Drop cookies

- **Chocolate chip cookies with red and green candies.** The classic, made festive.
- **Oatmeal cranberry cookies.** Chewy oats with dried cranberries and white chocolate.
- **Peanut butter blossoms.** Soft peanut butter cookies with a chocolate kiss pressed on top.
- **Cake mix cookies.** Boxed cake mix with eggs and oil, rolled in sprinkles.
- **Double chocolate cookies.** Fudgy, with a pinch of peppermint for the season.

### No-bake cookies

- **Chocolate peanut butter oat cookies.** Cooked on the stovetop and set on parchment.
- **Coconut snowballs.** Coconut, condensed milk and a roll in more coconut.
- **Pretzel kisses.** Mini pretzels topped with a melted chocolate candy and an M&M.
- **Chocolate-dipped sandwich cookies.** Store-bought cookies dipped and sprinkled.

## Which easy Christmas cookies are worth a little more time?

These take 45 minutes to an hour, often because the dough chills, but the steps stay simple.

### Classic holiday flavors

- **Butter shortbread.** Three main ingredients, pressed into a pan or sliced from a log.
- **Snowball cookies.** Buttery nut cookies rolled in powdered sugar while warm.
- **Gingerbread crinkles.** Molasses dough rolled in sugar, no cutters needed.
- **Chocolate crinkle cookies.** Rolled in powdered sugar so they crack as they bake.
- **Snickerdoodles.** Rolled in cinnamon sugar for a holiday smell.
- **Slice-and-bake sugar cookies.** Roll dough in sprinkles, chill, slice and bake.
- **Thumbprint cookies.** Press a dent and fill it with jam.

### Bars that count as cookies

- **Seven-layer bars.** A crumb base with layers of chips, coconut and condensed milk.
- **Sugar cookie bars.** Bake in a pan, frost and add sprinkles. No cutting shapes.
- **Gingerbread blondies.** All the spice of gingerbread in an easy pan.

## What are easy cookies for kids to help with?

Choose cookies with jobs little hands can do: rolling balls, pressing thumbprints, dipping and sprinkling.

### Kid-friendly ideas

- **Reindeer cookies.** Peanut butter cookies with pretzel antlers and candy noses.
- **Melted snowman cookies.** Round cookies with a white icing puddle and a marshmallow head.
- **Christmas tree pretzel rods.** Dipped in green candy melts and sprinkled.
- **Sprinkle-rolled sugar cookie balls.** Kids roll, you bake.
- **Hot cocoa cookies.** Chocolate cookies topped with a marshmallow.

## How do you get easy Christmas cookies right the first time?

A few habits fix most beginner problems.

### Beginner tips

1. **Measure flour by spooning it into the cup and leveling it.** Packing it in makes dry, crumbly cookies. Our [tablespoons in a cup guide](../how-many-tablespoons-in-a-cup/) helps when you need to measure small amounts.
2. **Soften butter, do not melt it,** unless the recipe says melted.
3. **Chill the dough** if it feels sticky or the cookies spread.
4. **Use parchment paper** and let baking sheets cool between batches.
5. **Pull cookies when edges are set.** They keep firming up on the hot pan.
6. **Do not eat raw dough** made with raw eggs or untreated flour.

## How far ahead can you bake Christmas cookies?

Baking ahead takes pressure off December.

### Storage guide

- **Room temperature:** most baked cookies keep about 1 week in an airtight container. Store crisp and soft cookies separately.
- **Freezer, baked:** up to 3 months. Freeze undecorated, then thaw and decorate.
- **Freezer, dough:** scoop into balls, freeze on a tray, then bag. Bake straight from frozen and add a minute or two.
