The best veg meal prep ideas are built on a few things you cook in bulk, like a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables and a protein such as chickpeas, lentils, tofu or eggs, then mix into different meals all week. Add a sauce or two and you can go from grain bowls to wraps to curry without cooking every night. Most of these keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge.

Below are 26 vegetarian meal prep ideas, with vegan options marked, plus a simple plan to prep them in one session.

## What makes vegetarian meal prep work?

Good veg meal prep has three parts in every container:

- **A protein.** Beans, lentils, chickpeas, tofu, tempeh, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese or paneer.
- **A grain or starch.** Rice, quinoa, farro, pasta, potatoes or tortillas.
- **Vegetables plus a sauce.** Roasted or raw vegetables and something that brings flavor, like tahini, peanut sauce, pesto or salsa.

Get those three right and your meals stay filling, so you're not hungry at 3 p.m. If you want to push protein further, our list of [high-protein meal prep ideas](../meal-prep-ideas-high-protein/) has more options, many of them meat-free.

## What are good vegetarian breakfast meal prep ideas?

Breakfast is the easiest meal to prep. Most of these take 15 minutes or less. For more, see our full guide to [breakfast meal prep ideas](../meal-prep-ideas-for-breakfast/).

1. **Overnight oats.** Oats, milk or plant milk, chia seeds and fruit. Vegan if you use plant milk.
2. **Egg muffin cups.** Whisked eggs with spinach, peppers and cheese, baked in a muffin tin.
3. **Chia pudding.** Chia seeds soaked in coconut or almond milk. Vegan.
4. **Greek yogurt parfaits.** Pack granola separately so it stays crunchy.
5. **Breakfast burritos.** Eggs, black beans, potatoes and salsa, wrapped and frozen.
6. **Tofu scramble.** Crumbled tofu with turmeric and vegetables. Vegan.
7. **Baked oatmeal squares.** Cut into portions and grab one on the way out.

## What are the best vegetarian lunch prep ideas?

Lunches should taste good cold or reheat well in a microwave.

8. **Chickpea grain bowls.** Quinoa, roasted chickpeas, cucumber, tomato and a lemon tahini dressing. Vegan.
9. **Mason jar salads.** Dressing on the bottom, sturdy vegetables and beans in the middle, greens on top.
10. **Lentil salad.** Lentils, roasted red peppers, feta and a mustard vinaigrette. Holds up for days.
11. **Black bean and corn burrito bowls.** Rice, beans, corn, peppers and salsa. Vegan without the cheese.
12. **Peanut noodle bowls.** Noodles, cabbage, carrots, edamame and peanut sauce. Vegan.
13. **Caprese pasta salad.** Pasta, mozzarella, tomatoes, basil and pesto.
14. **Hummus snack boxes.** Hummus, pita, carrots, cucumber, olives and a hard-boiled egg.

## What are easy vegetarian dinner prep ideas?

Dinners are where batch cooking pays off. Make one big pot and eat it twice, or freeze half.

15. **Vegetable and chickpea curry.** Freezes well and tastes even better on day two. Vegan.
16. **Three-bean chili.** A classic batch meal. Top with cheese or avocado. Vegan without the cheese.
17. **Red lentil soup.** Cheap, filling and ready in about 30 minutes. Vegan.
18. **Baked ziti with spinach.** Assemble in a 9x13 pan and portion out.
19. **Sheet-pan tofu and vegetables.** Tofu, broccoli and peppers roasted with soy and garlic. Vegan.
20. **Stuffed sweet potatoes.** Bake a batch and top with black beans, corn and salsa.
21. **Vegetable fried rice.** Use day-old rice and add eggs or tofu for protein.
22. **Palak paneer or chana masala with rice.** Rich, comforting and easy to reheat.

## What are good vegan and vegetarian snack prep ideas?

Snacks keep you from grabbing whatever's nearby in the afternoon.

23. **Roasted chickpeas.** Crunchy and salty. Store in an open container so they stay crisp. Vegan.
24. **Energy balls.** Oats, nut butter, honey or maple syrup, and chocolate chips. Vegan with maple.
25. **Veggie sticks with dip.** Carrots, peppers and celery with hummus or tzatziki.
26. **Hard-boiled eggs.** Peel them all at once and keep them in a container for up to a week.

## How do you meal prep vegetarian meals in one session?

You can prep a full week in about 2 hours if you work in this order.

### Step 1: Start the slow stuff (0 to 15 minutes)

- Put a pot of rice, quinoa or farro on the stove.
- Get a soup, chili or curry simmering.
- Preheat the oven.

### Step 2: Roast (15 to 60 minutes)

- Roast one or two trays of vegetables, like broccoli, sweet potatoes and peppers.
- Roast chickpeas or tofu on another tray.
- Bake egg muffins if you're making them.

### Step 3: Assemble (60 to 120 minutes)

- Whisk two sauces, like tahini lemon and peanut.
- Make overnight oats or chia pudding.
- Let hot food cool a bit, then portion it into containers. Refrigerate within 2 hours.

## How long does vegetarian meal prep last?

Here's a quick guide for common prepped foods stored in the fridge at 40°F or below.

| Food | Fridge | Freezer |
|---|---|---|
| Cooked rice and grains | 3 to 4 days | Up to 2 months |
| Cooked beans and lentils | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months |
| Bean chili, curry, lentil soup | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months |
| Roasted vegetables | 3 to 4 days | Not ideal, texture softens |
| Baked tofu | 3 to 4 days | Up to 2 months |
| Egg muffins | 3 to 4 days | Up to 2 months |
| Hard-boiled eggs (in shell) | Up to 1 week | Not recommended |
| Overnight oats | 3 to 4 days | Not recommended |

A few tips:

- Cool cooked rice quickly. Spread it on a tray, then refrigerate within 1 to 2 hours.
- Keep dressings and crunchy toppings separate until you eat.
- Freeze anything you won't eat by day 4, and reheat until steaming hot.
