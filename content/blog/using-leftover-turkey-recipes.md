The best leftover turkey recipes take cooked meat that's already seasoned and turn it into something that doesn't feel like a repeat of Thursday. Think soups, pot pies, enchiladas, fried rice and sandwiches that are better than the main event. Below are 24 ideas grouped by meal and mood, plus the safety basics so your leftovers stay delicious and safe all weekend.

First, one rule that matters more than any recipe: get the turkey into the fridge within 2 hours of serving, and plan to use it within 3 to 4 days.

## How long does leftover turkey last?

Cooked turkey keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge. That clock starts the day you cook it, not the day you carve the last slice.

### Store it the smart way

- **Carve it off the bone first.** Meat cools faster and takes less space in shallow containers.
- **Split it into dark and white meat.** Dark meat shines in soups and braises. White meat suits sandwiches and salads.
- **Add a splash of broth.** A spoonful in each container keeps the meat from drying out.
- **Label the date.** It sounds fussy, but on day four you'll be glad you did.

### Freeze what you won't eat by Monday

Freeze cooked turkey in 1 to 2 cup portions. That's about the amount most soups, casseroles and pastas call for. It tastes best within 2 to 6 months. Thaw it in the fridge overnight, never on the counter.

### Reheat it to 165°F

Whatever you make, reheat leftovers until they're steaming hot and reach 165°F (74°C) in the center. Bring soups and gravy to a rolling boil.

## What are the easiest leftover turkey recipes?

The easiest ideas need no recipe at all. You already have the hard part done.

### 10-minute lunches

1. **The day-after sandwich.** Turkey, stuffing, a smear of cranberry sauce and a little mayo on soft bread.
2. **Turkey melt.** Turkey and sharp cheddar on sourdough, grilled in butter until the cheese runs.
3. **Turkey quesadillas.** Shredded turkey, pepper jack and a spoonful of salsa folded into a tortilla and crisped in a pan.
4. **Cranberry turkey wraps.** Turkey, greens, a little cream cheese and cranberry sauce rolled up tight.
5. **Turkey salad.** Like chicken salad: chopped turkey, mayo or yogurt, celery, grapes or apple, and pecans.
6. **Turkey and avocado toast.** Thin slices on toast with mashed avocado, lemon and chili flakes.

### Quick weeknight dinners

7. **Turkey fried rice.** Day-old rice, diced turkey, frozen peas, egg and soy sauce in a hot pan.
8. **Turkey tacos.** Warm shredded turkey with chili powder and cumin, then pile on slaw and lime.
9. **Turkey pasta in a creamy sauce.** Toss with garlic, cream or broth, peas and parmesan.
10. **Turkey hash.** Crisp diced potatoes and onions, add turkey, and top with a fried egg.
11. **Turkey stir-fry.** Fast vegetables, a garlicky sauce and turkey added at the end just to heat through.

## How do you use every part of the turkey?

Leftovers aren't just the meat. A little planning makes the whole bird go further.

### Make stock from the carcass

Put the carcass in a big pot with an onion, a carrot, a couple of celery ribs and enough water to cover. Simmer gently for 3 to 4 hours, then strain. Cool it quickly, refrigerate it, and use it within 3 to 4 days, or freeze it in containers. That stock is the backbone of the soups above.

### Use the gravy and sides

Gravy works as the sauce for pot pie, shepherd's pie and open-faced sandwiches. Mashed potatoes become a topping or crispy potato cakes. Cranberry sauce is great stirred into yogurt or spread on a grilled cheese. Like turkey, cooked sides keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge.

## How do you plan a week of leftover turkey meals?

Leftovers go to waste when there's no plan. A rough week keeps you ahead of that 3 to 4 day window.

| Day | Idea | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| Friday | Day-after sandwiches | White meat, stuffing, cranberry |
| Saturday | Turkey noodle soup | Carcass stock, dark meat |
| Sunday | Turkey pot pie or enchiladas | Remaining meat, gravy |
| Monday | Freeze what's left | Portion into 1 to 2 cup bags |
